NovaCart Refund Policy

Overview
NovaCart offers refunds on eligible orders so that customers can shop with confidence. A refund is issued after a returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse, or after an order is cancelled before it ships.

Eligibility
Refunds are available for items returned within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is unused, in its original packaging, and includes all accessories and tags. Items that arrived damaged or defective are always eligible for a full refund, including shipping charges, if reported within 7 days of delivery.

Non-refundable items
The following items cannot be refunded: gift cards, downloadable software, personal care products that have been opened, and items marked "Final Sale" on the product page.

Refund method and timeline
Refunds are credited to the original payment method. Once the returned item passes inspection, the refund is processed within 2 business days. After processing, it usually takes:
- UPI and NovaCart Wallet: 1 to 3 business days
- Credit and debit cards: 5 to 7 business days
- Net banking: 3 to 5 business days
Cash on Delivery orders are refunded to the customer's bank account or NovaCart Wallet, as chosen by the customer.

Partial refunds
A partial refund may be issued if an item is returned with missing parts, signs of use, or damage that was not caused by NovaCart or the courier.

Shipping charges
Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is due to a NovaCart error, such as a wrong, damaged or defective item.

How to request a refund
Start a return from the "My Orders" page or ask the support assistant to create a support ticket. A refund is issued automatically once the return is approved.
