NovaCart Shipping and Delivery Policy

Shipping charges
Standard shipping is free on orders of 499 INR or more. Orders below 499 INR have a shipping charge of 49 INR. Express shipping costs 99 INR per order and is available in selected cities.

Delivery times
- Standard delivery: 3 to 7 business days
- Express delivery: 1 to 2 business days, in selected metro cities
- Remote areas: up to 10 business days
Delivery times start from the day the order is shipped, not the day it is placed. Orders are usually shipped within 1 to 2 business days of being placed.

Order tracking
After an order ships, a tracking update is sent by email and SMS. Customers can also ask the support assistant for the status of an order using the order ID.

Order statuses
- Processing: the order is being prepared and has not shipped yet.
- Shipped: the order has left the warehouse and is in transit.
- Out for Delivery: the courier will deliver the order today.
- Delivered: the order has been delivered.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled and will not be delivered.

Delayed deliveries
If an order has not arrived within 3 business days after its estimated delivery date, customers should contact support. The support assistant can create a support ticket so the logistics team can investigate with the courier.

Shipping locations
NovaCart currently ships only within India. International shipping is not available.
