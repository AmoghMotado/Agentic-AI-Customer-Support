NovaCart Payment Methods Policy

Accepted payment methods
NovaCart accepts the following payment methods:
- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and other UPI apps)
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express)
- Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay)
- Net banking from all major Indian banks
- NovaCart Wallet
- Cash on Delivery (COD)

Cash on Delivery
Cash on Delivery is available for orders up to 10,000 INR. A COD handling fee of 20 INR applies. COD is not available for gift cards.

EMI
No-cost EMI is available on selected credit cards for orders above 3,000 INR. EMI terms are shown on the checkout page.

Payment security
All online payments are processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. NovaCart never stores full card numbers and never asks for your card PIN, CVV or OTP over phone, email or chat.

Failed payments
If money is deducted but the order is not confirmed, the amount is automatically refunded within 5 to 7 business days. If it is not refunded in that time, contact support with the transaction reference.
