NovaCart Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact NovaCart support?
You can chat with the NovaCart support assistant at any time. Human support agents are available from 9 AM to 9 PM IST, every day. Support tickets are usually answered within 24 hours.

How do I track my order?
Ask the support assistant for the status of your order and provide your order ID, for example ORD1001. You can also see tracking details on the "My Orders" page.

Where can I find my order ID?
Your order ID starts with "ORD" followed by four digits. It is shown in the order confirmation email and on the "My Orders" page.

Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
The delivery address can be changed only while the order is in "Processing" status. Contact support with your order ID to request the change.

Do you offer gift wrapping?
Yes. Gift wrapping is available for 49 INR per item on selected products.

Do I need an account to place an order?
Yes. A NovaCart account is required so you can track orders, manage returns and receive refunds.

Is my personal information safe?
Yes. NovaCart uses encryption to protect personal data and does not sell customer information to third parties.

How do I get an invoice?
Invoices can be downloaded from the "My Orders" page after an order is shipped.
