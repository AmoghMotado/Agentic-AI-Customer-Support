NovaCart Warranty Policy

Manufacturer warranty
Most electronics and appliances sold on NovaCart include a manufacturer warranty. The warranty period is listed on the product page and is usually 1 year for electronics and 6 months for accessories.

What the warranty covers
The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures that occur during normal use within the warranty period.

What the warranty does not cover
The warranty does not cover physical damage, liquid damage, damage caused by misuse or unauthorized repairs, normal wear and tear, or consumable parts such as batteries after 6 months.

How to make a warranty claim
For most brands, warranty service is provided directly by the brand's authorized service centers. Customers should keep the NovaCart invoice, which is available on the "My Orders" page, as proof of purchase. If a customer needs help locating a service center or the brand rejects a valid claim, they can ask the support assistant to create a support ticket.

Defects within 7 days
If a product is defective within 7 days of delivery, it is handled by NovaCart under the Damaged or Defective Product Policy instead of the manufacturer warranty.
