NovaCart Return Policy

Return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Electronics such as phones, laptops and tablets have a shorter return window of 10 days from delivery.

Condition of returned items
Returned items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories, manuals, free gifts and tags. Electronics must be returned with the original box and must not be locked with a password or account.

Items that cannot be returned
Gift cards, opened personal care products, innerwear, perishable groceries, downloadable software, and items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.

How to return an item
1. Go to "My Orders" and select the item you want to return, or contact support.
2. Choose a reason for the return.
3. Schedule a free pickup or drop the package at a partner courier location.
4. Pack the item securely with the return slip included.

Return pickup
Return pickups are free for all eligible returns. Pickup is usually scheduled within 2 to 3 business days of the return request.

Exchanges
Exchanges are available for size or color changes on clothing and footwear, subject to stock availability. If the replacement is out of stock, a refund is issued instead.

Inspection
All returns are inspected at the warehouse within 2 business days of arrival. If an item fails inspection, it is shipped back to the customer and no refund is issued.
