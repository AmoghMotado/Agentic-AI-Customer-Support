NovaCart Damaged or Defective Product Policy

Reporting a damaged product
If an item arrives damaged, broken, defective, or different from what was ordered, the customer must report it within 7 days of delivery. Reports made after 7 days are handled under the Warranty Policy where applicable.

What to provide
To speed up the claim, customers should provide:
- The order ID
- A short description of the damage
- Photos of the damaged item and the outer packaging, if available

Resolution options
For a valid damage claim, the customer can choose one of the following:
- A free replacement of the same item, if in stock
- A full refund, including any shipping charges
Damaged items are collected through a free return pickup. In some low-value cases NovaCart may issue the refund without requiring the item to be returned.

Damaged package but item is fine
If the outer box is damaged but the item inside is undamaged, no claim is needed. Customers are still welcome to report it so we can give feedback to the courier.

How to raise a claim
Ask the support assistant to create a support ticket with the order ID and a description of the damage. A support agent reviews damage tickets within 24 hours.
