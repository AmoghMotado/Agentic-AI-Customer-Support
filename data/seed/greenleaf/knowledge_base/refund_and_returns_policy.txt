GreenLeaf Grocery Refund and Returns Policy

Fresh and perishable items
Fruits, vegetables, dairy, bread and meat cannot be returned. If a perishable item arrives spoiled, damaged or past its expiry date, report it within 48 hours of delivery with a photo, and we will refund it in full. No pickup is needed.

Packaged and non-perishable items
Sealed packaged goods such as rice, pulses, oil and snacks can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unopened.

How refunds are paid
Refunds go to the GreenLeaf Wallet instantly. Customers can ask for the refund to go to the original payment method instead, which takes 3 to 5 business days.

Missing items
If an item is missing from your order, report it within 24 hours and we will refund it to your GreenLeaf Wallet.
