GreenLeaf Grocery Orders and Payments FAQ

Can I cancel or change my order?
An order can be cancelled for free while its status is "Processing", which means packing has not finished. After the order is out for delivery it cannot be cancelled.

Which payment methods are accepted?
GreenLeaf accepts UPI, credit and debit cards, the GreenLeaf Wallet, and cash on delivery for orders up to 2,000 INR.

What is the GreenLeaf Fresh Pass?
Fresh Pass is a membership that costs 99 INR per month. Members get free delivery on every order with no minimum amount, and early access to same-day slots.

How do I contact GreenLeaf support?
You can chat with the GreenLeaf assistant at any time. Human support agents are available from 7 AM to 10 PM every day, and support tickets are usually answered within 6 hours.
